## Problem

<!-- Who had what problem, and why it mattered. 2–4 sentences. -->

Most workout-logging apps put progress statistics behind a premium subscription.
RepTracker is a free web app for logging exercises, sets and reps, with progress
statistics built in. It is web-based rather than a native app to avoid the cost of
publishing on the iOS App Store.

## Stack

<!-- One bullet per technology. Note anything used in an unusual or notable way. -->

- C# / .NET 10

- Entity Framework Core 10 (with Npgsql)
- PostgreSQL
- TypeScript
- React
- Vite
- Docker (multi-stage build) and Docker Compose
